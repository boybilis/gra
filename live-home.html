CERTIpass

Review Curriculum

The CERTIPass Curriculum is an expertly designed review program created to ensure that candidates pass their licensure exams with confidence. 

 

Combining high-yield content, real-world practice scenarios, and personalized guidance from top-tier Testmasters, CERTIPass equips students with the knowledge and strategies they need to succeed on their examinations and beyond.

 

This curriculum is perfect for those who have  failed multiple times or are aiming for success on their first attempt. 

Its flexible structure allows for comprehensive preparation at your own pace, and it’s tailored to guarantee first-time success.

Expert Instructor

Instruction by seasoned Testmasters who guide you through proven strategies to help you pass on your first attempt.

Interactive Learning

Engaging, interactive online sessions via Zoom, and computer-based study tools for an immersive learning experience.

High-Yield Content

Prioritizes the most critical concepts and topics frequently tested in the actual examinations, ensuring maximum impact with minimal study time.

Flexible Scheduling

Study at your own pace with both live sessions and 24/7 access to recorded lectures, so you can prepare anytime, anywhere.

Powered by artemis360

Our courses utilize the  Artemis360 LMS for Optimal Learning. 

 

At GRA, we are committed to providing a top-tier review experience, and that’s why our courses are powered by Artemis360. This advanced platform ensures that you have everything you need to succeed at your fingertips.

 

With Artemis360, you’ll enjoy:

  • 24/7 access to course materials – Watch recorded lectures, access study guides, and take practice exams whenever it’s convenient for you.
  • Interactive learning experience – Benefit from our advanced tools that make learning more engaging and effective.
  • Effective yet convenient test preparation – Review effectively with comfort, because Artemis360 tracks your progress as you go.

 

Our partnership with Artemis360 ensures that your review journey is streamlined, empowering you to reach your goals with confidence.
